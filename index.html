PSRDEX

Browse and analyse POLFAR pulsar observations at the Janusz Gil Institute of Astronomy.

PSRDEX is available only to users with access to the Kopernik server.
On the Kopernik/Kepler LAN, open the workspace directly:

Open PSRDEX on the LAN Server IP: 192.168.44.191:8501

Connecting from outside the LAN

1. Run this in a terminal on your own device:

ssh -X -J pmarmat@kepler.ia.uz.zgora.pl -L 8501:127.0.0.1:8501 pmarmat@kopernik

Use your authorized username on both hosts; pmarmat is shown here.

2. Keep the SSH session open, then visit http://localhost:8501/ on the same device.

Your device’s port 8501 → Kopernik’s port 8501, via Kepler.
127.0.0.1 means localhost. The -X option is optional for browser access.
