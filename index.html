Observation catalog

Browse pulsars, observing epochs and HBA/LBA coverage. Compare duration, bandwidth and time and frequency sampling before selecting archives.

Scattering analysis

Inspect pulse profiles, scattering-model fits and residuals. Study scattering timescales and their frequency dependence with recorded fit decisions.

Propagation diagnostics

Access dynamic spectra, autocorrelation and other PSRISM products. Review measurement resolution and quality before interpreting results.

Connecting through SSH or VPN

A VPN must provide a route and source address permitted by the institute’s network policy. SSH users can forward Kopernik’s port 8501 through their existing authorized connection and open the forwarded address in their browser. In VS Code Remote SSH, use the Ports panel.

This public page does not grant access to the archive, create an account, or start analysis jobs.
